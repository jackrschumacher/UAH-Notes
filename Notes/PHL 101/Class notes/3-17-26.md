- # Buddhism
	- ## 4 Truths
		- First truths are the conditions of existence
		1. Suffering is innate to the human existance
		2. The suffering that we go through as humans has a cause
			1. Suffer because of thirst 
			2. **Desire creates suffering**
		3. The suffering described in the first truth can be ended
		4. Noble eightfold path is the way away from the suffering described in the first truth
